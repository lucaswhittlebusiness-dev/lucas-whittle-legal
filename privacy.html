Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the personal stock research text-message service operated by Lucas Whittle ("Lucas Whittle", "we"). The service is for private personal use. The only recipient is its owner, Lucas Whittle.

What data we collect

The only personal data involved is the owner's own mobile phone number, which is used solely to deliver a daily research summary message on weekday mornings. No data is collected from any other person.

How it is used

The phone number is used only to send the owner's own messages through Twilio, our messaging provider. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Messages are delivered through Twilio, which processes them under its own privacy terms.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.
